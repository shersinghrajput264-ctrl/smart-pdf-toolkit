About Smart PDF Toolkit

Smart PDF Toolkit is a browser-based collection of simple PDF and image utilities. The goal is to make common file tasks easy on both mobile phones and computers.

Whenever possible, file processing happens locally in your browser. Files are not intentionally uploaded to our own server by these tools.

Privacy Policy

Smart PDF Toolkit provides browser-based file utilities. The tools on this page are designed to process selected files locally in your browser.

We do not intentionally collect the contents of files processed by the client-side tools.

Advertising services such as Google AdSense, when enabled, may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

This page is a starter privacy policy and should be reviewed and customized before using advertising or publishing the site commercially.

Terms of Use

  • Use the tools only for lawful purposes.
  • Do not upload or process files that you do not have permission to use.
  • Results may vary depending on the file and browser.
  • Always keep a backup of important documents.
  • The service is provided on an “as available” basis.
